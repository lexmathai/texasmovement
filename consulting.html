Frequently asked questions

What is included in the $1,000?

Up to four pages, responsive design, your branding, light content editing, contact actions, basic SEO, one revision round, launch assistance, and proper handover of all files.

When do the seven days begin?

The seven calendar days begin after scope is confirmed, deposit is received, all required content is received, and required access is provided.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, images, text content for each page, and any access needed for domain and hosting configuration.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain registration and hosting are separate costs that will be discussed during scoping.

Can you rebuild an existing site?

Yes, if it fits the scope. We'll review your current site during the scoping conversation.

Can I edit the site myself?

You can edit the HTML files directly or arrange for support. A visual CMS editor is not included in this build.

What costs extra?

Domain registration, hosting, additional pages, additional features, ongoing updates, and any paid third-party services.

What happens if I need more functionality?

We'll discuss whether it fits a revised scope and agreement. Some functionality may be outside what we offer.
